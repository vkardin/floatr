## Short description

Floating video player that remembers where you stopped: subtitles in PiP, bookmarks, Markdown export, speed and volume.

## Long description

Video Drift turns any web video into a floating window that stays on top while you work, read or chat, and remembers the exact second you stopped on every video.

SMART FLOATING PLAYER
• Document Picture-in-Picture window with Video Drift's own controls
• Subtitles drawn by the site (YouTube, Netflix, Video.js, Plyr, JW Player and more) stay with the video
• Falls back to native Picture-in-Picture or an in-page mini player you can drag and resize
• Choose which buttons the player shows

NEVER LOSE YOUR PLACE
• Playback position is saved automatically for every video
• "Continue from 12:34?" when you come back

BOOKMARKS AND FRAME NOTES
• Mark a moment with a note in one click or with Alt+Shift+B
• Export the notes of one video or all of them to a clean Markdown file for Obsidian, Notion or Logseq
• Copy a link that opens the video at the current timecode

QUICK CONTROL
• Shadow mode: opacity from 30% to 100% in the mini player over the page
• Playback speed 0.25×–4× and volume presets in the player and the popup
• Hotkeys for speed and bookmarks

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything is processed locally in your browser. No servers, no analytics, no trackers.

Interface in English, Russian and Ukrainian. Light and dark theme follow your system.
