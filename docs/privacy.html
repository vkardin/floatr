Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

In short

Floatr processes everything locally in your browser. It does not collect, send or sell any data. There are no servers, analytics, ads or trackers.

What is stored and where

  • Settings, bookmarks and frame notes: browser sync storage (chrome.storage.sync). If you use browser sync, your browser vendor syncs them between your devices.
  • Playback positions: local browser storage (chrome.storage.local) on this device only.
  • Markdown exports are created on your device and saved only where you choose.

Permissions

  • Access to pages: needed to find the video on the page, float it, read the current time and show the "Continue from" prompt.
  • storage: keeps your settings, bookmarks and positions.
  • activeTab: lets the popup talk to the tab you are on.

Deleting your data

Delete bookmarks in the popup or remove the extension: the browser then deletes all of its stored data.
